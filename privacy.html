Privacy Policy

ClipBot is a personal tool used by its owner to schedule and publish short videos to TikTok accounts the owner controls.

What we access

When an account is connected, ClipBot receives the account's basic profile information (display name) and permission to upload videos to that account. It does not access messages, followers, or any other data.

How it's used

Only to upload videos the owner has approved to the account that was connected.

Where it's stored

Login tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions. Removing it deletes ClipBot's ability to post.
